# Task 2 - Unsupervised Domain Adaptation

This directory implements the complete PACS Task 2 protocol: Source-only ERM,
DAN, DANN, CDAN, the common final evaluation, and the approved DAN
`lambda_MMD` study over `{0.1, 1, 10}`.

The Colab entry point is `notebooks/task2_domain_adaptation.ipynb`; its paired
percent-format source is retained for readable version-control diffs. Detailed
implementation reasoning is in `CODE_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md`, and frozen
experimental choices are in `DESIGN_DECISIONS.md`.

## Colab workflow

1. Open `notebooks/task2_domain_adaptation.ipynb` and select a T4-class GPU.
2. Run through the training and source-only diagnostic stages. The PACS archive
   is downloaded and verified, and the shared source split is saved.
3. Inspect the six selected runs and the experiment lock. Training never
   receives a Sketch label.
4. Change `RUN_FINAL_TARGET_EVALUATION` to `True` only after accepting the
   frozen configurations and checkpoints, then rerun from that cell.
5. Download `task2_results_seed6304.zip`. It includes all compact evidence, the
   split manifest, and the exact Source-only checkpoint required by Task 3.

## External attribution

- PACS originates from Li et al., *Deeper, Broader and Artier Domain
  Generalization* (ICCV 2017). The notebook uses the public PACS archive mirror
  also referenced by research benchmark download scripts; no dataset images are
  committed.
- DAN follows Long et al. (ICML 2015), DANN follows Ganin et al. (JMLR 2016),
  and CDAN follows Long et al. (NeurIPS 2018). The implementations here are
  assignment-specific and written around the common training pipeline rather
  than copied from an external repository.
